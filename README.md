# Rutger van Baren

Senior full-stack engineer in Amsterdam. Fifteen years of building products end to end — mostly the front-end, but I own the API and the infrastructure under it when that is what the product needs.

Most at home in **TypeScript** and **Node.js**: ten years of Angular, five of React and the TanStack ecosystem, Fastify and PostgreSQL on the server, and Rust with Axum for the parts that need it. I like taking technical ownership of a product: setting its direction, then making it fast and obvious to use.

Right now I run [NootropicsIndex](https://nootropicsindex.com), a price comparison platform I built and still maintain a TypeScript monorepo of eight services, a Playwright crawling pipeline across 16+ storefronts, and a gated classifier on a locally hosted LLM.

On the side I am currently modifying the firmware of my Samsung Odyssey OLED G9 (G93SC) to unlock advanced motion clarity features. Work in progress in the open at [g93sc-firmware-mod](https://github.com/rutgervanbaren/g93sc-firmware-mod). worth watching if you have the same panel.

Previously at [Bitonic](https://bitonic.nl), [FunXtion](https://funxtion.com), [BBN Adviseurs](https://bbn.nl), [Geodan](https://geodan.nl) and [NEP](https://nepgroup.com).
